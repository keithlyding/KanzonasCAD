| 0.9 | 7d90280 | Panel sheets and dimensions. | 0.5 | adb9dce | Contacts linked to their device, and cross-references (xrefs.py). The attributes box of a contact has "Belongs to" (devices listed, or a tag typed) and "List this contact under its device"; a coil's has "List its contacts under it" (attribute XREF = 0 to hide). Cross-references worked out from the drawing (cached per project state): under a device, one line per contact "NO 6-D3" in sheet then zone order; under each contact its device's location, or ? unlinked. Drawn by the renderer (screen, PDF) and written into DXF exports as text. Project setting "Update cross-references automatically" (on by default; off: every sheet keeps what it shows), per-sheet Freeze cross-references (Sheet.xref_frozen with the references it shows kept in Sheet.xref_shown), Update cross-references, and a status bar warning "Cross-references out of date" (click to update). Backups (backup.py): each Save first copies the previous file into "<name> backups" beside it, newest 10 kept (File > Open backups folder); autosave every 5 minutes of projects with unsaved changes into a recovery folder, removed on save or normal close, offered back at the next start after a crash. |
